Hi, I'm Riccardo, an engineering student based near Bologna, Italy. I hold a BSc in Automation Engineering from the University of Bologna (105/110), including an exchange year at Tongji University in Shanghai through the AlmaTong double degree. I'm now pursuing an MSc in Automation and Control Engineering at Politecnico di Milano.

My work sits between signals, control and hardware: extracting muscle synergies from sEMG for robotic hand control, building Python and ROS pipelines, designing a RISC-V CPU in Verilog, and programming PLCs in CODESYS.

Outside of the lab I'm a sporty guy who loves the gym and running, when not injured, haha.
